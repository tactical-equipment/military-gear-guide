Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can civilians legally own a plate carrier?

In most US states, civilians can buy and wear plate carriers and body armor. Some states restrict ownership for people with felony convictions, so check your local laws before you buy.

2. What size plate carrier should I buy?

Size the carrier to your plates, not your shirt. Most adults use 10x12 inch plates, and the carrier should sit high on the chest with the top plate edge near the collarbone notch.

3. What does military grade mean for civilian gear?

It usually means the item uses materials and construction similar to issued equipment, such as 500D or 1000D nylon and reinforced stitching. Always check the actual specs instead of relying on the label.

 

4. How should I store a loaded plate carrier?

Hang it upright on a wide, sturdy hanger rated for heavy loads. This keeps the shoulder straps from stretching, protects the plates and lets moisture dry out between uses.

5. Is tactical gear safe for kids?

Kid sized tactical clothing, packs and accessories are fine for play, camping and outdoor activities. Keep real armor and weapon related gear for adults, and focus kids’ kits on comfort and fit.
